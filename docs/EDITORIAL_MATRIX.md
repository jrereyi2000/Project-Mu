# Slot Targeting Rules

Some slots are pinned to a specific subject (team and/or game). The Editorial Desk resolves every targeted slot for the week **before** writers are assigned (`newsroom/slot_targets.py`), and injects the subject into the writer's prompt as a mandatory assignment. All targets for a week are resolved together, so **no team is the subject of more than one targeted slot in the same week**. Matchup primers are resolved first, then exit/elimination slots.

| Slots | Weeks | Subject rule |
| `LOCAL_MATCHUP_PRIMER` (Slots 6–8) | W1 | Opening week: 3x matchup primers on this week's scheduled games (byes excluded), ranked by **editorial interest** (projected Excitement Index, +3 for division games, +2 per team alive). Rotation: teams primed in previous 2 weeks skipped. Covers side with fewer primers this season (ties → popularity, home). Written by a beat reporter for that team. |
| `LOCAL_MATCHUP_PRIMER` (Slot 6: Crisis Beat; Slots 7–8: Primers) | W2–W18 | **Slot 6 (Crisis Beat / Hot Seat Accountability):** targets teams in structural crisis—the worst win-differential vs. expectation ($\Delta W = \text{Wins} - \text{Expected Wins} \le -2.0$) or active losing streaks with sub-.350 win percentages. Written by that team's beat reporter with a coaching crisis and accountability lens. If no team qualifies, falls back to the top marquee matchup primer. **Slots 7 & 8:** top remaining scheduled games ranked by editorial interest under standard rotation rules. |
| `LOCAL_MATCHUP_PRIMER` (Slot 8) | W19–W21 | The playoff game of the week with the highest projected Excitement Index, plus the division-game bonus if it applies (no rotation, no playoff-race bonus). |
| `LOCAL_MATCHUP_PRIMER` (Slots 6 & 7) | W23 | The Super Bowl: Slot 6 = AFC champion, Slot 7 = NFC champion. |
| `FRANCHISE_STATE_OF_THE_UNION` (Feature Trio) | W15–W18 | A **mathematically eliminated** team: at least 7 other conference teams already have more wins than the team can still reach (tiebreakers ignored, so a live team is never marked eliminated). Most popular first. If no team is eliminated yet, the worst record. |
| `COACHING_HOT_SEAT_AUDIT` (Slot 6) | W19 | The team that **missed the playoffs** (as below) whose head coach fell furthest short of roster expectation: wins − expected wins (`team_seasons.expected_wins`, Opponent-Adjusted Game Credit) **below −1.5**, steepest shortfall first (ties → more popular). It leads the Firing Line; the assignment also lists the other eliminated teams below −1.5. Resolved before the Exit slot. If no team qualifies, the slot runs untargeted. |
| `FRANCHISE_STATE_OF_THE_UNION` (Exit, Slot 7) | W19 | A team that **missed the playoffs**: not playing a Wild Card game and not its conference's #1-seed bye (best record among teams without a Wild Card game), and not the hot-seat subject. Most popular first. |
| `PLAYOFF_AUTOPSY` (Slots 6 & 7) | W20–W22 | Two distinct losers from the previous week's playoff round. Most popular first. |

All other slots are untargeted: the writer chooses the subject under the article type's curatorial autonomy. If league data is unavailable, or no subject qualifies, the slot runs untargeted.

**Power Rankings:** the Week 19 `POWER_RANKINGS` (entering the Wild Card round, through Week 18) is the season's final ranking. Weeks 20–23 publish no Power Rankings, so the regular-season order is never re-ranked during the playoffs.

**Week numbering:** postseason games are identified by week number (19 = Wild Card, 20 = Divisional, 21 = Conference Championships, 22 = Super Bowl bye, 23 = Super Bowl). Week 24 represents the unified Offseason Package (labeled "Offseason" in the newsroom edition selector).

# Roster Moves & Contracts

Writers may report the season's signings, releases, re-signings and restructures, and cite contract terms and cap figures, from the writer tools (`get_league_transactions`, `get_player_contract`, `get_team_cap_sheet`) and the assignment brief.

- **Relevance rule:** moves and cap figures appear **only when they matter to the article's subject**. Most articles carry no cap numbers at all. A move is worth a line when it changes the subject's lineup, depth chart or cap picture; otherwise leave it out.
- **No slot of their own:** in Weeks 1–23, moves get no slate slot (the one exception is *Trade Grades* below). They feed the existing slots: the national brief's notable moves, the beat brief's team moves, and the `CAP DATA:` / `ROSTER MOVES:` lines of the article types that use them.
- **Facts vs. colour:** that a move happened, its terms (years, total, this season's cap hit, bonus, cut savings, dead money, no-trade clause), team cap space, spend and dead money, rosters and contract end years come only from the tools and are never contradicted. Years and totals are stated as fact. A projected yearly figure (marked `est.`) is hedged in prose ("roughly", "projected", "about"); an estimated cap row is approximate. Never invented, even though untracked: guaranteed money, incentive details, option years. Free to invent: negotiation colour, anonymous front-office or agent quotes, motives, locker-room reaction, what the move means for the depth chart, and unnamed, unpriced interest from "other teams".
- **Trades:** a trade is reported as done only when it was executed. Anything else is a rumor and is described as one.
- **Rumors:** only from `get_trade_rumors` or the brief's `league_trades` (real talks between two front offices that didn't become a trade, through the Week 8 trade deadline). A rumor is reported only as talks, never as done, and never with a player's name or a figure it doesn't give. No other talks are described as actual talks.
- **Hypothetical packages** (`PLAYOFF_AUTOPSY`, `FRANCHISE_ARCHITECTURE_BLUEPRINT`): a writer may propose a package only as their own idea ("a deal they could chase"), never as reported talks, built from real players and picks with the terms the tools show, and only if it passes `check_trade_package` (the GMs' own legality check).
- **Trade Grades:** in Weeks 2–18, the edition after a week with an executed trade replaces Slot 5 of the Feature Trio with `TRADE_GRADES` (Mike Sando or Bill Barnwell), a letter grade for both sides of every trade executed the week before. Nothing else in the matrix changes.

## Narrative Anchor Dates & Temporal Cadence

Although all six stories are published together under `week = 24`, each article is researched, compiled, and narrated as if written in the week immediately following its respective event (`event_date + 1 week`). This decouples the publication edition (Week 24) from each story's narrative anchor:
- **Super Bowl Stories (Recap, Champion Retrospective, Runner-Up Postmortem):** Narrated from mid-February, immediately after the conclusion of Super Bowl Sunday.
- **Coaching Carousel Audit:** Narrated from late February, following the conclusion of the head coach and coordinator hiring cycle.
- **Marquee Free Agency Signing Grades:** Narrated from mid-March, following the opening wave of marquee unrestricted free-agent signings.
- **First-Round Draft Class Recap:** Narrated from early May, following the completion of the first round of the NFL Draft.

## Anti-Leakage Information Visibility Cascade

To preserve historical authenticity, prevent chronological leakage, and enforce narrative integrity, prompt contexts enforce a strict event-relative visibility cascade:

```
[Super Bowl (Week 23)]
        │
        ▼  (Sees season + SB game; NO coaching, offseason signings, or draft)
   [1. SB Recap, 2. Champion Retro, 3. Runner-Up Postmortem]
        │
        ▼  (Sees SB + coaching moves; NO offseason signings or draft)
   [4. Coaching Carousel Audit]
        │
        ▼  (Sees SB + coaching moves + signings; NO draft)
   [5. Marquee Signing Grades]
        │
        ▼  (Sees full offseason: SB + coaching + signings + Round 1 picks)
   [6. First-Round Draft Recap]
```

### Strict Anti-Leakage Rules:

1. **Super Bowl Articles (Recap, Champion Retrospective, Runner-Up Postmortem):**
   - Access concluded season data through Week 23 only.
   - Forbidden from referencing head coach firings, hires, coordinator movements, offseason free-agent departures/signings (moves after Week 23), or draft selections as completed facts. In-season signings and releases are part of the season and may be cited (e.g. a Week 9 signing who started in the Super Bowl).
2. **Coaching Carousel Audit:**
   - Access concluded season records, team situational efficiency, coach career profiles, and submitted coaching moves.
   - Forbidden from referencing offseason free-agent signings, offseason trades, or rookie draft selections. In-season moves are season history and may be cited.
   - **Coordinator & Scheme Rules:** Evaluates coordinator origins (promotions vs. outside hires) and coaching slants (`OFFENSIVE`, `DEFENSIVE`, `CEO`). Applies scheme inheritance rules: an offensive-slant hire imports offensive scheme/playbook while retaining defensive scheme continuity; a defensive-slant hire imports defensive scheme/playbook while retaining offensive scheme continuity; a CEO-slant hire delegates play-calling and schemes to retained/hired coordinators.
3. **Marquee Signing Grades:**
   - Access concluded season records, updated coaching/coordinator scheme context, and submitted marquee signing facts. Contract terms are cited from `get_player_contract` or the signing row; a term that's missing is never inferred.
   - Forbidden from referencing actual draft selections or rookie acquisitions.
   - **Strict Numeric OVR Ban:** Articles must NEVER state, quote, or cite numeric OVR ratings. Players are evaluated strictly through qualitative tiers:
     - **95–99 OVR:** *“Tier 1 superstar / elite franchise cornerstone”*
     - **90–94 OVR:** *“High-impact marquee starter / Pro Bowl caliber talent”*
   - **All-Pro Descriptor Rule:** The term "All-Pro" must NEVER be used as a generic synonym for high OVR. It may only be cited if the player earned an official AP All-Pro selection in the database `awards` table. Scope is strictly restricted to curated marquee players (90+ OVR).
4. **First-Round Draft Class Recap:**
   - Access full offseason context: coaching schemes, veteran signings, and captured actual Round 1 selections.
   - **Round 1 Only Constraint:** Strictly restricted to Round 1 selections (picks 1–32). Writers must never grade or imply coverage of Rounds 2–7, UDFAs, or full draft classes.
   - **Actual Results Only:** Actual picks originate exclusively from captured/ingested results in `draft_selections`, never mock projections (`mock_draft_picks`).
