## 5. Simulated Leagues: ADP and Rostered %

There are no real fantasy leagues, so drafts and availability are simulated: **100 fake 12-team PPR leagues run in parallel** each season (JR, Oct 13). They produce two things: a **real numerical ADP** from 100 drafts, and a weekly **rostered %**.

### 5.1 The draft (W1)

**What managers draft from.** Each manager drafts from the **consensus value board**: the `draft_score` that `engines/fantasy_adp_engine.py` already computes from OVR, team offense ratings, popularity and last season's awards. The managers can't draft from ADP, because ADP is now the *output* of these drafts. Drafting from it would be circular.

**Drafting strategies.** Every manager is assigned a strategy, which decides how they move off the value board:

| Strategy | How it drafts |
| :--- | :--- |
| `BPA` (Best Player Available) | Highest value on the board, subject only to roster limits |
| `HERO_RB` | One RB in rounds 1–2, then no RB until about round 6; WR/TE-heavy in between |
| `ZERO_RB` | No RB before about round 5; loads up on WR/TE/QB early, then takes volume RBs |
| `RB_HEAVY` | 2–3 RBs in the first 4 rounds |
| `EARLY_QB` | Takes an elite QB in rounds 1–3 |
| `EARLY_TE` | Takes an elite TE in rounds 1–3 |

- **Starting mix (decided, used for the first simulated season):** BPA 35%, Hero RB 15%, Zero RB 15%, RB Heavy 15%, Early QB 10%, Early TE 10%.
- **The mix adapts every season (decided).** Last season's strategy results set next season's league-wide mix across all 1,200 managers (100 leagues × 12). Results are **not** written up in any article. It's a partial move toward what worked, not a direct copy of title shares:
  - **Measure performance relative to how many managers used the strategy.** A strategy's *lift* = its share of titles ÷ its share of managers. BPA at 35% of managers winning 35% of titles has lift 1.0, which means average and no change.
  - **Damped update:** new share ∝ old share × lift^0.5, then blended 50/50 with the old mix and renormalized. For example, Zero RB at 15% of managers winning 60% of titles (lift 4.0) rises to about 25%, not 60%. 100 titles a season is a small sample, so damping keeps one lucky season from swinging the mix.
  - **Floor of 5% per strategy**, so no strategy dies out and can come back later.
  - Exponent, blend and floor are tunable. The engine stores each season's mix and the results that produced it.
- **Per-league noise (decided):** each league draws its own 12 strategies from the season mix, sampling seat by seat. Leagues therefore differ: one may have three Zero RB managers and another none. Only the league-wide average tracks the mix.
- **Every strategy also follows:**
  - roster construction: starting lineup QB / 2 RB / 2 WR / TE / FLEX, with K and D/ST once they exist, on a 15-round roster;
  - positional caps (no 4th QB);
  - a little noise per pick, so two BPA managers don't agree on every pick.
- **Reproducible:** fixed random seed per season.

**What the 100 drafts produce** (per player):
- **ADP** = the average pick number across all 100 drafts (e.g. 14.3). **In a league where the player goes undrafted, he counts as the pick after the last round** (pick 181 in a 12-team, 15-round draft; decided).
- **Earliest and latest pick** (e.g. "14.3 ADP, range 8–22").
- **Draft %**: the share of leagues where he was drafted at all.
- **ADP by strategy**: how early Zero RB managers take WRs compared with BPA managers. This is material for W1 articles.

These replace the current week 1 `adp_rank` source. `adp_rank` becomes the order by simulated ADP, so the ADP board and the ROS `TREND` keep working. New numbers: average pick, earliest pick, latest pick and draft %.

### 5.2 In season (after each week's games)

- Each team adds rising players and drops falling ones, using recent PPR, volume trend and ROS rank, plus bye-week and playoff-week streaming. Managers vary in how active they are, so leagues don't all make the same moves.
- **Rostered % = the share of the 100 leagues where the player is on a roster** that week (steps of 1%), stored per player per week. The week-to-week change shows who is being picked up.

### 5.3 Matchups, standings and playoffs (decided)

Each of the 100 leagues also plays its season:
- **Schedule:** 12-team round robin, Weeks 1–14.
- **Lineups:** each manager starts the highest-projected eligible players (QB / 2 RB / 2 WR / TE / FLEX; K and D/ST after phase 3). Byes are handled automatically. Week 1 uses the preseason value board, since there are no projections yet.
- **Scoring:** actual PPR from `vw_fantasy_scores`.
- **Standings:** win-loss record, ties broken by points for. Waiver priority follows reverse standings.
- **Playoffs: 6 teams.** Seeds 1–2 get a bye in Week 15, seeds 3–6 play quarterfinals in Week 15, semifinals in Week 16, final in Week 17.
- **Outputs:**
  - champion and final standings per league;
  - title rate, playoff rate and points for, by draft strategy. These feed next season's strategy mix (§5.1) and aren't published in articles;
  - **start %** per player per week (share of leagues where he was in a starting lineup).

### 5.4 Notes

- **Cost:** pure Python over data already in the database, with no Gemini calls. It needs only data that exists today, so it doesn't wait on the pipeline migration. K and D/ST join the drafts and rosters once their scoring exists (after phase 3).

## 6. Data Sources

**Available today:** `fantasy_projections` (wk 1 ADP; wk 2–18 projections, matchup stars, pos/ROS ranks), `vw_fantasy_scores`, `vw_fantasy_defense_vs_position`, Playoff SOS, `player_stats` box-score lines (pass/rush/rec counting stats, fumbles), roster (OVR, depth chart, age, experience).

**After pipeline migration phase 3** (`docs/PIPELINE_MIGRATION.md`): every game's box score and both teams' player stats are captured weekly. Season Stats is a cross-check only (Madden lists the top ~270 per category). New nullable columns come from the captured headers:
- Rushing: broken tackles (BTK), yards after contact (YAC), long. Receiving: run after catch (RAC), RAC average, long. Passing: sacks taken.
- Kicking: FG made/attempted by distance (29/39/49/50+), XP. **Kicker scoring becomes possible.**
- Defense per player: solo/assisted tackles, TFL, sacks, INT, deflections, forced/recovered fumbles, TDs, safeties. Added up by team with points allowed (from the score) and return TDs, this gives **D/ST scoring**. (Individual defensive players could support IDP, but IDP is out of scope.)
- Kick/punt returns: yards and TDs.
- Team: red zone offense and defense, 3rd/4th-down and 2-pt conversions, penalties.
- The roster capture adds dev trait, archetype and experience (used by W1 tiers).

**K and D/ST (decided: in after phase 3):** they need a scoring definition (e.g. standard FG-by-distance and XP for K; points allowed, sacks, takeaways, TDs and safeties for D/ST) and an extension of the projection engine. Today projections cover QB/RB/WR/TE only, and the migration keeps engines out of scope until it's done.

**Matchup audit (early 2036):** in 2035 the matchup factor added almost nothing to projection accuracy (backtest MAE comes almost entirely from per-game averages). But 2035 per-player lines were generated by splitting team totals, not captured. After about 6 weeks of real 2036 box scores, re-run `python3 -m engines.fantasy_projection_engine --backtest`, comparing with and without the matchup factor. If matchups still add nothing, cut `FANTASY_MATCHUP_EXPLOITS` or fold it into Start/Sit, and drop matchup weight from `FANTASY_DVP_REPORT` framing. If they help, keep it and consider retuning the matchup exponent.

**Not available or out of scope:** IDP (JR's call), injury news, targets, snaps, air yards, red-zone touches per player, DFS salaries, betting lines.
